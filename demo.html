How It Works
Step 1

A request comes in

A customer message, form submission, or booking request lands, no matter which channel it arrives from.

Step 2

NexOps checks and routes it

Availability, rules, and conditions are checked automatically, then the request is routed to the right workflow.

Step 3

Confirmation goes out

The customer gets an instant confirmation, and your team sees it logged, with zero manual follow-up.
